<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping, it's essential to consider your specific needs and preferences. Here are some key factors to think about:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Seat height and depth</strong>: Look for a chair with a seat height that allows your feet to rest flat on the floor or on a footrest, and a seat depth that allows you to sit with your back against the backrest.</li>
  <li><strong>Lumbar support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have a curved backrest that provides adequate lumbar support to prevent strain on your lower back.</li>
  <li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Adjustable armrests can help take pressure off your shoulders and neck, and allow you to work in a comfortable position.</li>
  <li><strong>Material and breathability</strong>: Consider a chair with a breathable fabric or mesh material to keep you cool and comfortable during long work hours.</li>
</ul>
