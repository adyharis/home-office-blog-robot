<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping for an ergonomic office chair, there are several key factors to consider. These include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Seat height and depth</strong>: The seat should be adjustable to fit your body, with a height that allows your feet to rest flat on the floor or on a footrest, and a depth that allows you to sit with your back against the backrest.</li>
<li><strong>Lumbar support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have a curved backrest that provides support for your lower back, promoting good posture and reducing strain on your muscles.</li>
<li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Adjustable armrests can help take pressure off your shoulders and neck, and should be adjustable to fit your body.</li>
<li><strong>Material and breathability</strong>: A breathable, durable material such as mesh or fabric can help keep you cool and comfortable during long periods of sitting.</li>
<li><strong>Adjustability</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should be highly adjustable, allowing you to customize the fit to your body.</li>
</ul>
