<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping, it's essential to consider a few key factors that will help you find the perfect chair for your needs. Here are some things to think about:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Seat height and depth</strong>: Look for a chair with a seat height that can be adjusted to fit your legs, and a depth that allows you to sit with your back against the backrest.</li>
  <li><strong>Lumbar support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have a curved backrest that provides support for your lower back.</li>
  <li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Adjustable armrests can help take pressure off your shoulders and neck.</li>
  <li><strong>Material and breathability</strong>: Consider a chair with a breathable mesh fabric or a cooling gel cushion to keep you comfortable during long work hours.</li>
</ul>
