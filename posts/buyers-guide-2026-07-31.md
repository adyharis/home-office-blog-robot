<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping, it's essential to consider a few key factors that will impact your decision. These include:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Seat Height and Depth</strong>: The seat should be adjustable to fit your legs, with a depth that allows you to sit with your back against the backrest and your knees at or below hip level.</li>
  <li><strong>Lumbar Support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have a lumbar support that fits your natural curve, promoting good posture and reducing strain on your lower back.</li>
  <li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Adjustable armrests can help take pressure off your shoulders and neck, but make sure they're not too high or too low for your body.</li>
  <li><strong>Material and Breathability</strong>: Consider a chair with a breathable material, such as mesh, to keep you cool and comfortable during long work sessions.</li>
</ul>
