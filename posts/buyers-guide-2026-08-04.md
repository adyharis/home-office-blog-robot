<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping, it's essential to think about your specific needs and preferences. Here are some key factors to consider:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Adjustability</strong>: Look for a chair that allows you to adjust the height, armrests, and tilt to fit your body.</li>
  <li><strong>Lumbar Support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have a lumbar support system that promotes good posture and reduces strain on your lower back.</li>
  <li><strong>Cushioning and Breathability</strong>: A comfortable seat and backrest are essential, and breathable materials like mesh can help keep you cool and dry.</li>
  <li><strong>Durability</strong>: Consider the quality of the materials and construction, and look for a chair with a sturdy frame and reliable mechanisms.</li>
</ul>
