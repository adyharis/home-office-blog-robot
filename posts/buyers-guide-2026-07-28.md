<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping, it's essential to think about your specific needs and preferences. Here are some key factors to consider:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Seat Height and Depth</strong>: Look for a chair with a seat height that allows your feet to rest flat on the floor or on a footrest, and a seat depth that allows you to sit with your back against the backrest.</li>
  <li><strong>Lumbar Support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have adjustable lumbar support to help maintain the natural curve of your lower back.</li>
  <li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Adjustable armrests can help take pressure off your shoulders and neck, and allow you to sit with your arms at a 90-degree angle.</li>
  <li><strong>Material and Breathability</strong>: Consider a chair with a breathable material, such as mesh, to keep you cool and comfortable during long work hours.</li>
</ul>
