<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before making a purchase, consider the following factors:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Adjustability</strong>: Look for a chair that allows you to adjust the height, armrests, and lumbar support to fit your body.</li>
  <li><strong>Material and Breathability</strong>: Choose a chair with a breathable material, such as mesh, to keep you cool and comfortable during long working hours.</li>
  <li><strong>Seat Depth and Width</strong>: Ensure the seat is deep and wide enough to support your thighs and allow you to sit with your back against the lumbar support.</li>
  <li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Consider a chair with adjustable armrests to support your arms and reduce strain on your shoulders and neck.</li>
  <li><strong>Warranty and Durability</strong>: Check the warranty and durability of the chair to ensure it will last for a long time.</li>
</ul>
