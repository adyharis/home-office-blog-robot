<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before you start shopping for an ergonomic office chair, there are several factors to keep in mind. These include:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Seat Height and Depth</strong>: The seat should be adjustable to fit your leg length, with your feet flat on the floor or on a footrest, and your knees at or below hip level.</li>
<li><strong>Lumbar Support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should have a lumbar adjustment to fit your natural curve, providing support for your lower back.</li>
<li><strong>Armrests</strong>: Armrests should be adjustable in height and width to allow you to rest your arms at a 90-degree angle, with your elbows close to your body.</li>
<li><strong>Material and Breathability</strong>: Consider a chair with a breathable fabric, such as mesh, to keep you cool and comfortable during long work hours.</li>
<li><strong>Adjustability</strong>: Look for a chair with multiple adjustment options, including seat height, armrest height, and tilt, to customize the fit to your body.</li>
</ul>
