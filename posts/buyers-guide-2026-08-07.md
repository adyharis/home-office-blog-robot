<h2>Key Factors to Consider</h2>
<p>Before we dive into some specific product recommendations, let's take a look at some of the key factors you should consider when shopping for an ergonomic office chair. These include:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Adjustability</strong>: Look for a chair that allows you to adjust the height, armrests, and lumbar support to fit your body.</li>
  <li><strong>Support</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should provide adequate support for your lower back, as well as your legs and feet.</li>
  <li><strong>Comfort</strong>: Consider the type of cushioning and upholstery used in the chair, as well as the overall design and aesthetics.</li>
  <li><strong>Durability</strong>: A good ergonomic chair should be built to last, with a sturdy frame and high-quality materials.</li>
</ul>
